FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions regarding Agritech services

What is Agritech?

Agritech is a digital marketplace for agriculture where farmers, FPOs, traders, and agribusinesses can buy and sell agri-produce, inputs, and services with complete transparency, fair pricing, and nationwide reach.

What products and services are available?

Agritech offers: Trading of crops, grains, pulses, and oilseeds Agri-inputs like seeds, fertilizers, and pesticides IoT-based farm devices such as soil testing kits, weather sensors, and smart irrigation tools Agri logistics, warehousing, and quality testing services

How does Agritech help farmers and FPOs?

Agritech helps by: Connecting them directly with verified buyers Offering better price discovery and instant market access Providing digital tools like IoT soil testing and yield analysis Supporting end-to-end trade, including logistics and payments

What is the Agritech Auction feature?

The Agritech Auction feature allows farmers, FPOs, and traders to sell their agricultural produce through online bidding. Buyers can view live listings, place bids in real time, and purchase crops at the best market-driven price.

How does an auction work on Agritech Platform?

Seller lists the product for auction (with crop details, quantity, base price, and duration). The auction goes live at a scheduled time. Registered buyers place bids during the auction period. The highest bidder at auction close wins the product. Agritech facilitates communication, logistics, and payment between buyer and seller.
