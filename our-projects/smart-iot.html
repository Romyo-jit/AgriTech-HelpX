Smart IoT Devices for Precision Farming

We are piloting IoT sensor networks that include soil moisture, ambient climate, and plant health monitoring systems across partner farms. These sensors continuously capture real-time field data such as temperature, humidity, soil nutrients, and crop growth indicators, which are then analysed by our AI-based analytics engine.

This enables precision irrigation, nutrient management, and early detection of pest or disease risks, helping farmers make informed decisions to improve productivity and reduce input costs.

By combining AI insights with on-ground IoT intelligence, Agritech supports predictive and sustainable farm management, promoting better resource utilisation, higher yields, and improved profitability. These initiatives are aligned with emerging global trends in IoT and AI-driven agriculture, paving the way for scalable, climate-smart farming solutions suited to Indian conditions.
